QGenda Improver

Adds each shift's hospital location and travel time to your QGenda calendar, so your iPhone can send "Time to Leave" alerts.

In QGenda, copy the iCal subscription link for your schedule.
The calendar event starts this much earlier than your shift. The real shift time is added to the event notes.

Your new calendar link
Subscribe on iPhone
Tap Subscribe on your iPhone and confirm in Calendar. Or in Calendar, choose Add Calendar → Add Subscription Calendar and paste the link. Remove your old QGenda subscription afterward to avoid duplicates.

Your QGenda link works like a password for your schedule, and the new link contains it. Don't share the new link publicly. Nothing is stored on a server; the link carries your settings.
